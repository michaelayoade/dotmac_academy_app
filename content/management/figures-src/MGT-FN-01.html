400 customers is a ₦720,000 loss; 600 is a ₦920,000 profit. A 50% rise in volume swung profit by ₦1.64m — and the same leverage runs in reverse, which is why a high-fixed-cost business losing a fifth of its customers is in more trouble than "a fifth" sounds.
