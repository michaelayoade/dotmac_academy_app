Questions

Before you apply

Do I need experience to apply?

No. The entrance assessment measures where you are today — numeracy, reading, reasoning — and places you accordingly. Effort and consistency do the rest.

How is the training delivered?

Through the academy platform: structured chapters you read at pace, hands-on lab environments, chapter tests as practice, and final assessments that gate certification.

Are the certificates real?

Every certificate carries a verification ID that anyone — including an employer — can check on this site. If it can't be verified, we didn't issue it.

What happens after I apply?

You'll receive an email with your personal assessment link. Sit it when ready; results, offers, and onboarding follow automatically from your score.
