Questions

Before you start

Do I need experience for the technical academy?

No. The entrance assessment measures where you are today — numeracy, reading, reasoning — and places you accordingly. Effort and consistency do the rest.

Who are the management courses for?

Anyone who leads work or people — supervisors, team leads, managers, business owners. The principles are industry-neutral; no technical background is needed or assumed.

Are the certificates real?

Every certificate carries a verification ID that anyone — including an employer — can check on this site. If it can't be verified, we didn't issue it.

How do I enroll my team in the management courses?

Write to {{ management_contact_email | default("fiberacademy@dotmac.ng") }} — enrollment is set up per team or individual, and invitations arrive by email with everything needed to start.
