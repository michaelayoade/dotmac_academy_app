Free ICT training for NYSC corps members

Dotmac Technical Academy offers free technical training to eligible National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) members who want to build practical ICT and networking skills during their service year.

Eligible corps members can combine free online training in ICT and networking with practical sessions during their service year.

What ICT skills can corps members learn?

Browse fibre engineering, wireless and radio, routing and switching, network operations, and technical support in the course catalogue.

How does training fit around NYSC?

The Academy offers self-paced online study and practical training. Confirm the practical schedule and location before applying. If you need a Place of Primary Assignment (PPA), check separately with the Academy and your NYSC office.

How do I apply for NYSC ICT training?

Choose a training track on the application form and follow the emailed instructions for your entrance assessment.
