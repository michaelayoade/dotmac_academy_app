Free ICT and networking training for SIWES students

Eligible students completing the Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) can apply for free technical training at Dotmac Technical Academy.

The programme combines free online training for eligible SIWES students with hands-on ICT and networking practice.

What can SIWES students learn at Dotmac?

Explore tracks in fibre engineering, wireless networks, routing and switching, and network operations. The Academy combines self-paced online study with practical training.

Can this count towards my industrial attachment?

Check with your school’s SIWES coordinator and the Academy before relying on the programme for placement credit. Confirm any introduction letter, logbook, supervision or completion documentation your school requires.

How do I apply for SIWES ICT training?

Choose a training track on the application form and follow the emailed instructions for your entrance assessment.
