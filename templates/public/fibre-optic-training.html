Fibre optic training in Nigeria

Learn about fibre networks through Dotmac Technical Academy’s Fiber Engineering course. Working technicians and other applicants outside formal education can apply to build their fibre and networking skills. The course has 32 chapters, chapter tests and a final assessment.

Eligible applicants can access free online training as part of the technical programme. The Fiber Engineering track combines self-paced study with hands-on fibre training.

Who can apply for free fibre optic training?

Dotmac Technical Academy offers free technical training to eligible SIWES students, NYSC corps members and OND/HND interns. Confirm Fiber Engineering availability for your intake before applying.

Is fibre optic training fully online?

The technical programme combines self-paced online study with hands-on learning. Check the current intake for practical dates, location and equipment requirements.

How do I apply for Fiber Engineering?

Choose a training track on the application form and follow the emailed instructions for your entrance assessment.

Apply for Fiber Engineering. See the SIWES training page or NYSC training page for details relevant to your application.
